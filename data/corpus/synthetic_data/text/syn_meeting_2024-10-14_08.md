## Discussion

### 1. FY2025 Bookings Outlook & Revenue Targets
- **Director, Finance & FP&A** opened with a recap of the current pipeline. The internal bookings target for fiscal 2025 is $75 million to $95 million, and the team is tracking toward the lower half of that range through Q2 close.
- **Chief Revenue Officer** noted that three enterprise deals currently in late-stage negotiation could meaningfully shift the number toward the midpoint. Emphasis was placed on accelerating legal review cycles to avoid Q4 slippage.
- **Account Lead, Enterprise Channels** flagged that one government-adjacent contract may require additional compliance documentation, potentially pushing signature into Q1 FY2026. CRO to assess risk to year-end bookings number.
- Decision: Finance to publish a refreshed weekly pipeline dashboard by October 18, shared with all attendees.

### 2. 2025 Hiring Plan Update — Control Electronics
- **VP People & Talent** presented the approved headcount plan. The 2025 hiring plan calls for 40 additional control-electronics engineers to support expanded system builds and next-generation trap development.
- **VP Hardware Engineering** confirmed that requisitions for the first cohort (approximately 15 roles) are ready to post pending final job-description sign-off from Legal. Target start dates for this cohort are Q1 2025.
- Discussion around sourcing strategy: university partnerships (MIT, University of Maryland, Georgia Tech) and targeted LinkedIn campaigns were identified as primary channels. A referral bonus uplift for specialized RF/analog roles was proposed.
- **VP People & Talent** to coordinate with Director, Program Management on a phased onboarding plan so new engineers are productive against hardware milestones without overloading existing team leads.
- Action item: VP People & Talent to finalize job postings and submit to Legal by October 21, 2024.

### 3. Vendor Security Questionnaire Status
- **Director, IT & Security** reported that 7 of 11 active vendors have returned completed security questionnaires under the updated SOC 2 alignment framework. Outstanding responses are from three cloud-infrastructure sub-processors and one hardware component supplier.
- Escalation emails were sent October 11; responses are due October 25. Any vendor failing to respond by that date will be escalated to Procurement Manager for contract-compliance review.
- **Procurement Manager** noted that one supplier (a cryogenic component vendor) has indicated they will need a two-week extension due to an internal audit cycle. Extension granted to November 8, 2024, with written acknowledgment required.
- Decision: IT & Security to maintain a live tracker in Confluence and brief leadership on any material gaps at the next biweekly ops review.

### 4. Aria-Class System Calibration Cadence Review
- **VP Hardware Engineering** presented a summary of the current calibration schedule for Aria-class systems deployed in production and cloud-access environments.
- Current cadence is a full recalibration every 72 hours with intermediate drift checks every 8 hours. Data from the past 90 days indicates the 8-hour drift check is catching correctable deviations in approximately 12% of cycles, validating the schedule.
- A proposal to extend the full recalibration window to 96 hours for stable systems was discussed. CTO expressed support contingent on a 30-day monitored trial with tightened drift thresholds.
- Decision: VP Hardware Engineering to design a 30-day pilot protocol for two nominated Aria-class nodes and report findings at the November program review.
