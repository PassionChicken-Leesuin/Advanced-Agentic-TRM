## Discussion

### 1. Quarterly OKR Review (Q3 Actuals / Q4 Outlook)
- Director of Finance presented Q3 OKR scorecard. Three of five company-level key results were rated green; two (cloud bookings growth, enterprise pipeline conversion) were rated yellow.
- CEO noted that Q4 pipeline looks stronger given recent partnership announcements and requested that the VP of Software & Algorithms align roadmap milestones to the two lagging KRs before the next board update on 12 November.
- VP of Engineering confirmed that hardware delivery timelines are tracking to plan and do not represent a bottleneck for Q4 OKR attainment.
- **Decision:** Q4 OKR mid-cycle check-in scheduled for 5 November; Director of Finance to distribute updated tracker by EOD 1 November.

### 2. Tempo Program Status Update
- VP of Hardware provided a detailed status briefing on the Tempo system development program.
- The confirmed target specification for Tempo is **64 algorithmic qubits with 99.9% two-qubit gate fidelity, supported by approximately 100 physical qubits**. VP of Hardware confirmed this specification remains unchanged from the roadmap baseline approved in Q2.
- Fabrication of the ion trap modules is on schedule. First trap-set delivery from the supplier is expected in late November 2024.
- CTO raised a question about thermal management margins at the target qubit count; VP of Hardware confirmed that simulations show adequate margin and that empirical validation will occur during the Q1 2025 bring-up phase.
- **Decision:** Tempo specification document (Rev. 3) to be formally signed off by VP of Hardware and CTO by 31 October 2024.

### 3. Metrics & Benchmarking Strategy
- VP of Marketing & Communications presented a proposal on external performance communication strategy for 2025.
- **Marketing proposed retaining #AQ (Algorithmic Qubit) as the primary headline performance metric through the end of 2025, and committing to publishing the full #AQ benchmark circuits along with raw results for every new system released.** The rationale is that #AQ has gained meaningful recognition among enterprise customers and the research community, and a metric change mid-cycle would introduce confusion.
- VP of Software & Algorithms expressed support, noting that full transparency via published circuits and raw data will strengthen credibility with academic collaborators.
- CTO asked whether publishing raw results creates any competitive IP risk; VP of Marketing & Communications confirmed that legal reviewed the proposal and found no material IP concern.
- Director of Product Management will coordinate with the software team to build an automated results-publishing pipeline to reduce manual overhead each release cycle.
- **Decision:** #AQ benchmarking transparency proposal approved. VP of Marketing & Communications to draft external communications plan by 8 November 2024.

### 4. Data Retention Policy Update
- Senior Director of Operations briefed the group on proposed revisions to IonQ's internal data retention policy, driven by updated guidance from external legal counsel and evolving state-level data privacy regulations.
- Key change: raw experimental telemetry data from quantum systems will now be retained for a minimum of 36 months (up from 24 months) to support longitudinal performance analysis and regulatory audit readiness.
- Customer-facing job result data retention windows remain governed by individual contractual terms and are unaffected by this policy change.
- **Decision:** Revised data retention policy (v2.4) approved pending final sign-off from General Counsel. Senior Director of Operations to circulate for signature by 29 October 2024.
