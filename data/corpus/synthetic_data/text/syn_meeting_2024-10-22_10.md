## 1. Quarterly OKR Review

- Head of Finance opened with a summary of Q3 OKR attainment. Overall company score landed at approximately 74%, slightly below the 80% target threshold.

## 2. Tempo Program Status Update

- VP Hardware Engineering provided a detailed status briefing on the Tempo system development roadmap.
- The current target specification for Tempo calls for 64 algorithmic qubits and approximately 100 physical qubits, with a two-qubit gate fidelity target of 99.9%. The team confirmed these specifications remain unchanged and engineering milestones are tracking to plan.
- Ion trap fabrication yield has improved to 91% over the last two production runs, which is ahead of schedule.
- Director of Program Management noted that the cryogenic subsystem vendor has confirmed on-time delivery for the Q1 2025 integration window.
- Open risk: RF control electronics lead time remains at 18 weeks; procurement order must be placed no later than November 1 to avoid schedule impact.
- **Action Item:** VP Hardware Engineering to confirm purchase order issuance with Procurement by October 28.

---

## 3. Metrics Strategy – AQ and Beyond

- VP Marketing & Communications presented a proposal regarding the future of IonQ's Algorithmic Qubit (#AQ) metric in external communications.
- Marketing proposed retiring the #AQ metric following the AQ 64 milestone and transitioning to reporting in terms of physical and logical qubit counts beginning in 2025, with the rationale that the broader quantum computing industry is expected to converge on logical-qubit-based metrics as the standard for system comparison.
- CTO expressed support for the direction, noting it aligns with how enterprise customers and research partners are beginning to frame procurement discussions.
- VP Software & Algorithms raised the concern that some existing customer contracts and benchmarking agreements reference AQ explicitly; legal review will be needed before any public-facing change.
- CEO confirmed the strategic intent to lead rather than follow on metrics standardization.
- **Decision:** Marketing to draft a transition communications plan for review at the November leadership meeting. Legal to be looped in by November 1 regarding contract language implications.
- **Action Item:** VP Marketing & Communications to circulate draft metrics transition plan by November 12.

---

## 4. Data Retention Policy Update

- IT Operations Lead presented proposed revisions to the company's internal data retention policy, driven by updated compliance requirements under the company's federal contract obligations.
- Key changes include extending retention of engineering test logs from 3 years to 5 years, and requiring encrypted archival of all customer-facing simulation outputs within 30 days of generation.
- The revised policy will apply to all internal systems including the lab data management platform and cloud storage environments.
- **Decision:** Revised data retention policy approved in principle; formal sign-off pending review by General Counsel. Target effective date: December 1, 2024.
- **Action Item:** IT Operations Lead to submit final policy document to General Counsel by November 8.
