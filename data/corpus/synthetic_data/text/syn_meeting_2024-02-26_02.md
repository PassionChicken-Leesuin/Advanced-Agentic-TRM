### 2. Northfield Pharma Pilot — Requirements Review

The Account Lead, Life Sciences, presented the finalized technical requirements received from Northfield Pharma for their drug-binding simulation pilot, which is scoped for the second half of 2025.

**Key discussion points:**
- Northfield Pharma requires a two-qubit gate fidelity of at least 99.9% and a minimum of 60 algorithmic qubits for the drug-binding simulation pilot planned for H2 2025. The Account Lead confirmed these thresholds were non-negotiable and have been formally communicated in writing.
- Director of Hardware noted that current Forte 1 production benchmarks are approaching but have not yet consistently sustained 99.9% two-qubit gate fidelity across 60+ algorithmic qubits at production load. A dedicated fidelity improvement workstream is already underway.
- VP of Engineering directed the Hardware and Software teams to produce a joint readiness assessment by **2024-03-15** confirming whether the Forte Enterprise roadmap can satisfy Northfield's thresholds ahead of H2 2025 go-live.
- Senior Program Manager, Enterprise Accounts, will schedule a technical alignment call with the Northfield Pharma science team for early March.

**Decision:** Northfield Pharma pilot will be tracked as a Tier-1 program. Monthly internal milestone reviews to begin in March.

---

### 3. AWS Braket / Forte Enterprise Integration Update

- Account Lead, Cloud Partnerships, reported that during the most recent AWS Braket integration sync, the AWS Braket team formally requested that job queue latency for Forte Enterprise workloads remain under 10 minutes end-to-end.
- Director of Software noted that current median latency is approximately 13–15 minutes under peak load conditions and that the scheduler patch deployed on 2024-02-20 is expected to reduce this, though full benchmarking results are not yet available.
- A target of sub-10-minute queue latency has been added to the Forte Enterprise Q2 2024 performance objectives.
- Director of Software to deliver latency benchmark report by **2024-03-08**.

**Decision:** Formal latency SLA language incorporating the sub-10-minute threshold will be included in the next AWS Braket contract amendment draft.

---

### 4. Aria-Class Systems — Calibration Cadence

- Head of Systems Calibration proposed moving Aria-class systems from a 48-hour full recalibration cycle to a 36-hour cycle following observed drift patterns identified in January data.
- Lab Operations Manager raised staffing concerns for overnight calibration windows; agreed to pilot the 36-hour cadence on Aria Unit 2 only, beginning **2024-03-04**, with a four-week review period.
- All calibration logs must be uploaded to the shared LIMS portal within two hours of cycle completion.

**Decision:** Pilot approved for Aria Unit 2. Full fleet rollout contingent on pilot outcomes.

### 5. Lab Safety Training Compliance

- Lab Operations Manager reported that 14 of 22 lab staff have completed the updated Q1 2024 laser safety module. Deadline for completion is **2024-03-01**.
- Remaining 8 staff members have been sent reminder notices. Any personnel not certified by deadline will be temporarily restricted from laser-active lab areas per IonQ safety policy.
- Cryogenic handling refresher training is scheduled for **2024-03-07**; attendance is mandatory for all hardware technicians.
