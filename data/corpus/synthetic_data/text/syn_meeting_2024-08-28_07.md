## Discussion

### 1. Strategic Update: Post-Quantum Cryptography Market Opportunity
- VP Business Development presented a strategy memo circulated to leadership on August 26, which argues that NIST's finalization of post-quantum cryptography standards in August 2024 will shift enterprise security budgets, creating an opening for a bundled quantum-networking and QKD offer.
- The memo identifies financial services, federal contractors, and critical infrastructure operators as the highest-priority verticals for this bundled offering.
- Director of Quantum Networking noted that several prospective customers have already reached out following NIST's announcement, citing compliance timelines as a driver.
- CEO directed VP Business Development to develop a formal go-to-market proposal by September 30, including pricing models and partnership options for the QKD hardware layer.
- CTO cautioned that the messaging must clearly distinguish IonQ's hardware roadmap from software-only post-quantum solutions to avoid customer confusion.
- **Decision:** Proceed with scoping a bundled quantum-networking and QKD commercial package. VP Business Development to own. Target first customer briefing in Q4 2024.

---

### 2. 2027 Error-Correction Roadmap and Qubit Overhead Assumptions
- Director of Error Correction Research presented updated planning assumptions for the 2027 fault-tolerant system milestone.
- Current planning assumes a physical-to-logical qubit ratio of approximately 12.5 to 1 using QLDPC codes by 2027, which the team considers achievable given projected gate fidelity improvements.
- VP Hardware Engineering confirmed that the trap fabrication roadmap is being revised to accommodate the qubit counts implied by this overhead ratio.
- VP Software & Algorithms flagged that decoder latency at this code distance remains a risk; a dedicated working group will be formed to address real-time decoding performance.
- Finance Lead requested that the updated overhead ratio be reflected in the capital expenditure model for FY2025–2027 before the next board review.
- **Decision:** Adopt 12.5:1 physical-to-logical ratio using QLDPC codes as the baseline planning assumption for 2027 roadmap documents. Director of Error Correction Research to update roadmap deck by September 13.

---

### 3. Noise Characterization Study Status
- VP Hardware Engineering reported that the ongoing noise characterization study across the College Park trap systems is approximately 70% complete.
- Preliminary findings indicate that motional heating rates in the newer blade-trap modules are roughly 15% lower than in the previous generation, which is consistent with modeling predictions.
- A full report including crosstalk and leakage error profiles is expected by September 20.
- Senior Program Manager noted that the study results will feed directly into the next scheduled #AQ benchmark run; timing should be coordinated with the Software team.
- **Action:** VP Hardware Engineering to share draft noise characterization report with VP Software & Algorithms by September 18 for integration planning.

---

### 4. Patent Disclosure Backlog Review
- Head of Intellectual Property reported a current backlog of 14 undocketed invention disclosures, with the oldest submission dating to late June 2024.
- Primary bottlenecks are reviewer availability in the error-correction and photonics teams and a pending external counsel capacity issue.
- Head of IP is engaging a second outside patent firm to supplement throughput; engagement letter expected by September 6.
- CEO emphasized that protecting IP related to the QLDPC decoder work and the QKD integration architecture is a near-term priority.
- **Decision:** Head of IP to triage the 14 disclosures by strategic priority and present a clearance schedule at the September 11 program review.
