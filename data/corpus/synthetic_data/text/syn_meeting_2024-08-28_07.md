## Discussion Notes

### 1. Quantum Networking & QKD Commercial Strategy Update

- **VP Business Development** presented a strategy memo circulated earlier this week arguing that NIST's finalization of post-quantum cryptography standards in August 2024 will shift enterprise security budgets, creating a clear opening for a bundled quantum-networking and QKD offer from IonQ.
- The memo identifies financial services, defense contractors, and critical infrastructure operators as the primary near-term verticals. Several of these segments are already under board-level pressure to demonstrate cryptographic migration plans.
- **Director of Quantum Networking** confirmed that the QKD subsystem prototype is on track for an internal demonstration in Q4 2024. Integration with IonQ's trapped-ion platform remains the critical path item.
- **CTO** cautioned that the bundled offer narrative must be technically defensible before it is used in customer conversations. Marketing materials should not outpace hardware readiness.
- **Decision:** VP Business Development to develop a one-page customer-facing brief for the bundled networking/QKD concept, subject to CTO and Director of Product Management review before any external distribution. Target completion: September 20, 2024.

### 2. Error-Correction Roadmap and Logical Qubit Planning

- **VP Software & Algorithms** provided an update on the fault-tolerant computing planning horizon. Current planning assumes error-corrected logical qubits will use physical-to-logical encoding ratios in the range of 16:1 to 32:1, which is consistent with the overhead projections published in IonQ's 2020 public roadmap.
- Discussion centered on how these encoding assumptions affect near-term hardware targets. The VP Hardware Engineering noted that achieving the lower end of that range (16:1) is a gating milestone for the first logical qubit demonstrations and drives trap size, gate fidelity, and connectivity requirements.
- **CTO** asked the VP Software & Algorithms to prepare a sensitivity analysis showing how algorithm performance scales across the 16:1 to 32:1 range for two benchmark applications. This will inform the next board update.
- **Decision:** Sensitivity analysis to be delivered to the CTO by September 13, 2024.

### 3. Noise Characterization Study Status

- **VP Hardware Engineering** reported that the noise characterization study currently underway is approximately 60% complete. The team is analyzing crosstalk and motional heating rates across the latest generation trap.
- Preliminary results indicate motional heating rates are within acceptable bounds, but two-qubit gate crosstalk at higher ion-chain lengths is showing higher variance than modeled. The team is investigating whether this is a trap fabrication artifact or a control electronics issue.
- A full report is expected by September 6, 2024. The Senior Program Manager will schedule a dedicated technical review session once the report is available.
- **Action Item:** VP Hardware Engineering to circulate preliminary findings summary to the CTO and VP Software & Algorithms by September 3, 2024.

### 4. Patent Disclosure Backlog

- **Head of IP & Legal** flagged that the current patent disclosure backlog stands at 14 disclosures awaiting engineering review, with the oldest submission dating to early June 2024. This is above the target threshold of 8 open disclosures at any time.
- Root cause identified as competing priorities during the Q2 system release cycle. The Head of IP & Legal requested that each VP nominate a technical reviewer from their team to help clear the backlog by end of September.
- **Decision:** Each VP to identify a designated patent review liaison by September 6, 2024. Head of IP & Legal to coordinate scheduling.
