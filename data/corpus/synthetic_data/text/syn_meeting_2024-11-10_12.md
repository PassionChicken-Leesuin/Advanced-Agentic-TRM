**Discussion**

**1. Q4 2024 Vendor Screening – Multi-Channel Acousto-Optic Modulators**

The Procurement Manager opened with a summary of the ongoing component sourcing effort. The Q4 2024 vendor screening process has shortlisted three suppliers of multi-channel acousto-optic modulators, including a German supplier referred to internally as Vendor B. The VP Hardware noted that Vendor B's preliminary spec sheets show favorable channel isolation figures, though lead times remain a concern given current transatlantic logistics constraints. The Senior Program Manager requested that a technical evaluation matrix be circulated to the hardware team by end of week. The Procurement Manager confirmed that NDAs are in place with all three shortlisted vendors and that bench evaluation units can be requested in Q1 2025 pending leadership sign-off.

- **Decision:** Proceed with formal RFQ to all three shortlisted vendors; hardware team to provide evaluation criteria by November 15.
- **Action:** Procurement Manager to issue RFQs no later than November 22, 2024.

---

**2. Quantum Networking Product Roadmap Review**

The Director of Quantum Networking presented an update on the mid-term product roadmap. A key milestone discussed was the planned launch of a "quantum networking starter kit" — a bundled product combining QKD hardware and an ion-photon entanglement node — which is targeted for availability in 2026. The Director of Product Management emphasized that the 2026 target is contingent on hitting internal fidelity benchmarks for the entanglement node by Q3 2025 and securing at least two design-partner commitments in the first half of next year. The VP Hardware raised questions about photonic interconnect packaging, which the Director of Quantum Networking agreed to address in a dedicated technical review scheduled for late November.

- **Decision:** Quantum networking starter kit roadmap entry to be formally added to the 2025–2026 product plan document.
- **Action:** Director of Quantum Networking to draft product requirements document (PRD) draft by December 6, 2024.

---

**3. Compiler Optimization Pass – Status and Blockers**

The VP Software reported that the latest compiler optimization pass targeting two-qubit gate reduction is approximately 70% complete. The primary blocker is a regression identified in the routing layer affecting circuits with more than 12 qubits. A patch is in review and expected to merge by November 14. The team is on track to deliver the full optimization pass to the internal benchmark suite before the end of November. No resource escalation was requested at this time.

- **Action:** VP Software to confirm merge and regression closure by November 15 and update the software dashboard accordingly.

---

**4. Cloud Job Scheduler Maintenance Window**

The Cloud Infrastructure Lead notified attendees that a scheduled maintenance window for the cloud job scheduler is planned for **Saturday, November 16, 2024, from 02:00–06:00 AM ET**. During this window, job submission will be suspended and any queued jobs will be held and resumed automatically post-maintenance. The purpose of the maintenance is to deploy a revised priority-queue algorithm and update dependency libraries. Customer-facing status page notifications will go out 48 hours in advance. Internal teams running automated benchmark pipelines were advised to pause scheduled jobs before 01:45 AM on November 16.

- **Action:** Cloud Infrastructure Lead to send internal notification to all engineering leads by EOD November 13.
