**1. Q4 M&A Screening Update — Electronic Qubit Control**

VP Corporate Development presented results of the Q4 2024 screening exercise focused on inorganic opportunities in electronic qubit control technology. The process has shortlisted three acquisition candidates, designated internally as Candidate A, Candidate B, and Candidate C for confidentiality purposes. Candidate B is a UK-based startup with proprietary low-latency FPGA control stack architecture that the team flagged as particularly differentiated.

- CTO noted that Candidate B's control latency benchmarks align well with IonQ's near-term gate fidelity improvement targets.
- VP Corporate Development confirmed NDAs are in place for all three candidates; preliminary financial due diligence is scheduled to begin in late November.
- CEO directed the team to prioritize technical due diligence for Candidate B given the strategic fit, while keeping parallel tracks open for Candidates A and C.
- **Decision:** Engage external legal counsel to begin structuring term sheet language for Candidate B by November 22, 2024.
- **Action Item:** VP Corporate Development to schedule technical deep-dive with Candidate B engineering team — due November 20, 2024.

**2. Quantum Networking Product Roadmap — 2026 Starter Kit**

Director, Quantum Networking Programs provided a roadmap update. The team is targeting a 2026 commercial launch of a 'quantum networking starter kit,' a bundled product that will combine QKD (quantum key distribution) hardware with a quantum memory node in a single integrated offering aimed at enterprise and government customers.

- Director noted that the starter kit framing is intended to lower the barrier to entry for customers evaluating quantum-secure communications infrastructure.
- CTO raised questions about quantum memory coherence time requirements; Director confirmed the current spec targets >1 ms storage fidelity, which is achievable with the planned trapped-ion memory node.
- VP Hardware Engineering flagged that supply chain planning for the QKD optical components needs to begin no later than Q2 2025 to hit the 2026 window.
- **Decision:** Formal product requirements document (PRD) for the starter kit to be circulated for leadership review by December 13, 2024.
- **Action Item:** Director, Quantum Networking Programs to draft PRD outline — due November 25, 2024.

**3. Compiler Optimization Pass Status**

Head of Compiler Engineering reported that the new two-qubit gate decomposition optimization pass has completed internal benchmarking. Results show an average 12% reduction in circuit depth across a representative test suite of 200 circuits.

- Team is targeting a staged rollout: internal systems first (week of November 18), followed by cloud-accessible systems (week of December 2).
- One regression was identified in circuits using mid-circuit measurement; a fix is in review and expected to merge by November 15.
- **Action Item:** Head of Compiler Engineering to circulate benchmark summary report to CTO and VP Software & Cloud — due November 14, 2024.
