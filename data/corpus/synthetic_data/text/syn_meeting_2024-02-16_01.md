## Discussion

### 1. Enterprise Market Outlook & On-Premise Deployment Roadmap
- VP, Enterprise & Government Sales opened with a summary of the latest internal market sizing exercise. The internal estimate projects the on-premise enterprise quantum computing market will reach **$1.2 billion by 2028**, driven primarily by defense, financial services, and pharmaceutical verticals seeking air-gapped or sovereign deployments.
- The team agreed this figure should anchor the FY25 budget justification for the enterprise systems product line currently in planning.
- Program Manager, Enterprise Deployments noted that at least three prospective customers have already issued RFIs specifically requesting on-premise configurations; responses are due Q2 2024.
- VP, Hardware Engineering cautioned that on-premise system packaging and cryogenic servicing logistics remain open engineering risks that must be resolved before broad commercialization.

### 2. Interconnect Program — Frequency Converter Supply Chain
- Director, Quantum Networking reported that the team has completed preliminary design selection for the photonic interconnect module and is moving to vendor qualification.
- **Helion Photonics** has been engaged as the preferred supplier for the 1550 nm to visible frequency converters required in the interconnect signal chain. Helion Photonics has quoted a **26-week lead time** for these components, which the Director flagged as the single longest lead-time item in the interconnect bill of materials.
- Manager, Supply Chain & Procurement confirmed that a 26-week lead time, if orders are not placed by early March 2024, will push first-article delivery into Q3 2024 and risk slipping the interconnect integration milestone.
- Discussion: The team debated whether to place a preliminary purchase order against the current design revision or wait for final specification sign-off. Decision deferred pending engineering review (see Action Items).
- VP, Hardware Engineering asked whether alternative suppliers exist. Director, Quantum Networking confirmed that two other vendors were surveyed but neither could meet the optical efficiency specifications at the required wavelength conversion ratio.

### 3. Benchmark Reproducibility Checklist — Status Review
- Manager, Benchmarking & Performance Validation presented draft v0.4 of the benchmark reproducibility checklist, which formalizes the pre-run verification steps required before any algorithmic benchmark result is logged as a program record.
- Key additions in v0.4: mandatory ion chain recrystallization confirmation, gate calibration timestamp delta check (must be ≤4 hours prior to benchmark run), and environmental sensor log attachment.
- The checklist was generally well received. Director, Systems Integration requested one additional field capturing the trap loading success rate for the session.
- Target for v0.5 review: 01 March 2024.

### 4. Laser Alignment Procedure Revision
- Senior Manager, Photonics & Laser Systems presented the revised laser alignment procedure (LAP-2024-03), incorporating lessons learned from two alignment incidents in Q4 2023 that resulted in extended downtime.
- Key changes: two-person verification step added at the final mirror adjustment stage; beam profiler measurement now required before and after any tip-tilt adjustment; updated safety interlock confirmation sequence.
- VP, Hardware Engineering approved LAP-2024-03 for immediate adoption in the College Park lab. Rollout to the Bothell facility will follow after local team training, targeted for 29 February 2024.

### 5. Prior Action Items
- All prior action items from 02 February were either closed or carried forward per the tracker; no blocking items noted.
