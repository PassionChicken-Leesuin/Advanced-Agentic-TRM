## 1. Q3 2024 Technology Assessment – Electronic Gate Control

**Presenter:** CTO

- The CTO opened by framing the central open question from the Q3 2024 technology assessment: whether IonQ should build electronic (RF/microwave) gate control capabilities in-house, or obtain that capability through licensing or acquisition.
- This decision has been flagged as a strategic priority given the direct impact on gate fidelity roadmap targets for 2026 and beyond.
- VP Hardware Engineering presented Engineering's current workload estimates. Engineering estimates that standing up a full in-house electronic-control program would take at least four years, accounting for hiring, toolchain development, and validation cycles, whereas pursuing an acquisition path could deliver comparable capability in roughly eighteen months.
- The Director of Corporate Development noted that two potential acquisition targets have been identified for preliminary diligence; NDAs are pending legal review. No names are to be circulated outside this group at this stage.
- The CEO emphasized that cost modeling for both paths must be completed before a recommendation is brought to the Board. A side-by-side financial analysis is needed.
- Discussion surfaced a hybrid option: license existing IP to accelerate near-term work while a longer-term in-house capability is built in parallel. The CTO agreed to include this scenario in the formal assessment writeup.

**Decisions:**
- No path selected at this time; assessment remains open pending financial modeling.
- CTO to circulate a structured options memo by August 23, 2024.

## 2. SDK 1.4 Release Status & Notes

**Presenter:** VP Software & SDK

- SDK version 1.4.0 entered release candidate stage on August 7. Final regression suite is 94% complete as of this morning.
- Key changes in 1.4: updated noise model API (breaking change for users on 1.3.x), improved circuit optimization pass reducing two-qubit gate count by an average of 8% on benchmark circuits, and expanded support for mid-circuit measurement on Forte-class systems.
- Documentation team is finalizing migration guide for 1.3 → 1.4; expected publication concurrent with GA release.
- GA target remains August 19, 2024, contingent on no Sev-1 issues surfacing in the remaining regression runs.
- VP Software noted that the developer relations team will post changelog highlights to the IonQ developer portal on release day.

**Action Items:**
| Owner | Action | Due Date |
|---|---|---|
| VP Software & SDK | Confirm regression completion and green-light GA | Aug 16, 2024 |
| Program Ops Manager | Coordinate internal comms for SDK 1.4 GA announcement | Aug 18, 2024 |
