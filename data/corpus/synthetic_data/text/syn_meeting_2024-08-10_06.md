**1. Q3 2024 Technology Assessment – Beam Delivery Architecture**

The CTO opened by framing the central unresolved item carried forward from the Q3 2024 technology assessment: whether to retain free-space laser beam delivery for the next system generation, or to transition to integrated photonic beam delivery directly on the trap chip. This remains an open question requiring a decision before the architecture freeze, currently targeted for Q4 2024.

- **VP Hardware Engineering** presented a comparative summary of the two pathways. Engineering estimates that integrated photonic beam delivery would take approximately three years to reach production readiness, whereas upgrading the existing acousto-optic modulator (AOM) system would require roughly one year to reach a deployable state.
- The CTO noted that the three-year timeline for integrated photonics is not disqualifying in isolation, but must be weighed against customer delivery commitments and the competitive landscape. A near-term AOM upgrade could serve as a bridge while the photonics roadmap matures.
- **Head of Quantum Control Engineering** flagged that the AOM upgrade path still yields meaningful fidelity improvements and would not preclude a later transition to integrated photonics.
- **Director of Systems Integration** raised concerns about chip-level thermal management if photonic components are integrated on-trap, and requested that a thermal modeling study be scoped.
- **Program Manager, Next-Generation Systems** confirmed that the architecture decision gate is blocking two downstream workstreams. A decision or an explicit deferral with a revised timeline is needed by September 30.

*Decision:* No final architecture decision today. CTO to convene a dedicated technical working session by August 28 with Hardware Engineering, Control Engineering, and Systems Integration to produce a recommendation for leadership.

**2. SDK v1.4 Release Status & Notes**

- **VP Software & SDK** reported that SDK v1.4 entered final regression testing on August 7. Key additions include improved circuit compilation for mid-circuit measurement workflows, updated noise model parameters reflecting the latest hardware characterization data, and expanded Python 3.12 compatibility.
- One blocker remains: a reproducible serialization error in the Qiskit transpiler plugin under edge-case circuit depths. Engineering is targeting a fix by August 14, keeping the planned August 19 external release on track.
- Documentation updates and changelog are complete pending the fix. Release notes will be distributed internally 48 hours before publication.

*Action Item:* VP Software & SDK to confirm go/no-go status to the CTO by EOD August 15.

**3. Facilities HVAC Upgrade – Status Update**

- **Director of Facilities & Operations** reported that Phase 1 of the HVAC upgrade in the Lab Wing (Bays 3–5) was completed on August 2, on schedule and within budget.
- Phase 2, covering the main server corridor and the optical table lab, is scheduled to begin August 19. A planned 6-hour maintenance window will be required; the Director confirmed coordination with lab leads to avoid impact to active experiments.
