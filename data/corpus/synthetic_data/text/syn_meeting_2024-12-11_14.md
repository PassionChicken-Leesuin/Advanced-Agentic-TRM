## 1. Hardware Supply Chain Update

**Presenter:** Director of Supply Chain & Procurement

- The Director confirmed that a letter of intent with Nordic Quantum Fabrication for a second source of trap chips was signed in November 2024, providing IonQ with a critical redundancy path for trap chip supply ahead of anticipated 2025 system build ramp.
- Legal is currently finalizing the full supply agreement; target execution date is Q1 2025. The Senior Program Manager, Systems Integration will coordinate qualification testing milestones with the vendor.
- The VP Hardware Engineering noted that incoming wafer samples from Nordic Quantum Fabrication are expected in late January 2025 and will be evaluated against IonQ's internal trap chip acceptance spec (rev. 4.2). A parallel qualification run with the existing primary supplier will continue through Q2 2025.
- **Decision:** Procurement to draft a dual-source risk matrix by December 20, 2024, for CTO review prior to the January Board update.
- **Action Item:** Director of Supply Chain & Procurement to schedule a technical alignment call with Nordic Quantum Fabrication engineering contacts by December 18, 2024.

## 2. 2025 Workforce Planning

**Presenter:** Director of Talent Acquisition

- The Director of Talent Acquisition presented the approved 2025 headcount plan. The 2025 hiring plan calls for 40 additional control-electronics engineers, reflecting the scaling requirements tied to next-generation system deployments and the increased complexity of multi-zone trap control architectures.
- Requisitions are being staged for opening in January 2025, with a target of 50% of the 40 roles filled by end of Q2 2025. Sourcing strategy will prioritize candidates with RF/microwave mixed-signal experience.
- The CTO emphasized that onboarding timelines must be coordinated with the hardware roadmap so that new engineers can contribute meaningfully to the Forte Enterprise build schedule.
- Compensation benchmarking against peer quantum and semiconductor firms is complete; offers will be competitive at the senior and staff levels.
- **Action Item:** Director of Talent Acquisition to circulate draft job descriptions to VP Hardware Engineering for technical review by December 17, 2024.

## 3. SDK v1.3 Release Readiness

**Presenter:** VP Software & SDK

- SDK v1.3 is targeting a public release on December 19, 2024. Key changes include: updated noise model parameters for Forte-class systems, improved circuit compilation pass ordering for two-qubit gate depth reduction (~12% average on standard benchmarks), and expanded support for the OpenQASM 3.0 `stretch` directive.
- The VP Software & SDK noted that internal regression testing passed on December 9, 2024, with no P1 defects open. Two P2 issues related to circuit visualization edge cases are deferred to v1.3.1.
- Release notes draft has been shared with the Cloud & Partnerships team for customer-facing communication alignment.
- **Decision:** Release to proceed on December 19, 2024, pending sign-off from Head of Quality & Reliability by December 16, 2024.

## 4. Benchmark Reproducibility Checklist Review

**Presenter:** Head of Quality & Reliability

- The team reviewed the updated benchmark reproducibility checklist (BRC rev. 2.1), which now includes: system calibration state logging prior to each benchmark run, mandatory thermal stabilization wait period (≥ 45 minutes post-cooldown), seed-fixed random circuit generation for algorithmic benchmarks, and automated cross-run variance reporting with a ±2% tolerance gate.
- The checklist will be mandatory for all externally reported results starting January 1, 2025. Internal teams should begin dry runs immediately.
- **Action Item:** Senior Program Manager, Systems Integration to distribute BRC rev. 2.1 to all hardware and software leads by December 13, 2024.
