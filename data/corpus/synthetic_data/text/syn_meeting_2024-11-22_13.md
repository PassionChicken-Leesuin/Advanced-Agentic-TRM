## Discussion

### 1. Hardware Supply Chain Update
- **Director, Supply Chain & Procurement** reported that a letter of intent with Nordic Quantum Fabrication for a second source of trap chips was signed in November 2024, reducing single-supplier risk for the company's ion-trap chip pipeline.
- The CTO noted that qualification testing timelines with Nordic Quantum Fabrication will be integrated into the FY2025 hardware roadmap review scheduled for January.
- VP Hardware Engineering requested that a formal supplier quality plan be drafted by end of Q4 2024. **Action → Director, Supply Chain & Procurement by 13 December 2024.**

### 2. Export Compliance & Government Customer Status
- **Director, Government Programs** confirmed that an export license application for the Forte system destined for KISTI in Korea was filed in September 2024 and that the application remains under active review with the relevant regulatory authority.
- The team agreed to schedule a biweekly check-in between Government Programs and Legal to monitor status and ensure any requests for additional information are addressed within 72 hours of receipt. **Action → Director, Government Programs to establish cadence by 29 November 2024.**
- CEO noted the KISTI deployment is a high-visibility international reference account and asked VP Business Development to keep the account plan current pending license outcome.

### 3. Forte Enterprise Product Specification Review
- **VP Hardware Engineering** walked the group through the finalized on-premise specification for Forte Enterprise: the system fits within two standard 19-inch racks, consumes approximately 3 kW of power, requires no cryogenics, and delivers 36 algorithmic qubits.
- The CTO emphasized that the no-cryogenics design is a key differentiator for data-center and government facility deployments and should be prominently featured in updated collateral.
- VP Software & Algorithms confirmed that the 36 algorithmic qubit target is validated against current benchmarking suite results. Minor calibration refinements are still in progress.
- **Action → VP Business Development** to coordinate with Marketing on refreshed one-pager reflecting the confirmed specs by **6 December 2024.**

### 4. Intern Program Onboarding Planning (Spring 2025)
- **Director, People Operations** presented a draft onboarding schedule for the Spring 2025 intern cohort (anticipated start: 13 January 2025).
- Key discussion points: lab access badge provisioning lead time is 10 business days and must be initiated at offer acceptance; all interns require completion of export control training before accessing controlled hardware areas.
- IT confirmed that intern account provisioning workflow has been updated to flag export control training as a hard prerequisite gate. **Action → Director, People Operations** to circulate updated onboarding checklist to hiring managers by **29 November 2024.**

### 5. Data Retention Policy Update
- **Senior Manager, IT & Compliance** presented proposed revisions to the company's data retention policy, primarily to align with updated contractual obligations from two federal customers.
- Proposed changes include extending retention periods for certain instrument log files from 3 years to 7 years and formalizing a quarterly deletion audit for data past its retention window.
- Legal review is pending; the revised policy is targeted for Board-level ratification at the December 2024 Audit Committee meeting. **Action → Senior Manager, IT & Compliance** to deliver redlined policy to General Counsel by **2 December 2024.**

### 6. Open Items / AOB
- CEO reminded all VP-level leaders that FY2025 budget submissions are due to Finance by **6 December 2024**; no extensions will be granted.
- Next leadership program meeting scheduled for **Friday, 6 December 2024, 10:00 AM ET.**

1. Nordic Quantum Fabrication LOI acknowledged; supplier quality plan to be initiated immediately.
2. KISTI export license monitoring cadence approved.
3. Forte Enterprise on-premise specification (two 19-inch racks, ~3 kW, no cryogenics, 36 AQ) confirmed as final for external collateral.
4. Spring 2025 intern onboarding checklist to be updated with export control gate.
5. Data retention policy revision to proceed to General Counsel review.
