## Discussion

### 1. Tempo Program Status Update
- **Program Manager, Tempo** opened with a schedule review. The team had previously targeted a Q1 2025 date for the first customer shipment; however, **the first Tempo customer shipment has slipped to Q2 2025 due to a delay from the laser subsystem supplier.** The supplier cited component lead-time issues affecting optical bench integration.
- **VP Hardware Engineering** confirmed that the engineering team has been in daily contact with the supplier and is evaluating a secondary source to reduce single-vendor risk going forward. A formal risk-mitigation memo will be circulated by October 25.
- **Director, Supply Chain** noted that contractual SLA clauses are being reviewed with Legal to assess remedies. No additional downstream system dependencies are currently impacted beyond the initial ship date.
- **CTO** emphasized the importance of transparent customer communication. **VP Operations** will coordinate with the Account Management team to draft updated delivery timelines for affected customers by October 28.
- **Decision:** Approve a secondary laser subsystem supplier qualification program. VP Hardware Engineering to own. Target qualification complete by end of Q1 2025.

### 2. 2025 Hiring Plan Review
- **Director, People Operations** presented the approved 2025 headcount plan. Among the most significant additions, **the 2025 hiring plan calls for 40 additional control-electronics engineers**, reflecting the company's commitment to scaling hardware capabilities in support of Tempo and next-generation system development.
- **Finance Business Partner** confirmed that budget for these roles has been earmarked in the 2025 operating plan and that requisitions can open in phased tranches beginning January 2025.
- **VP Hardware Engineering** flagged that sourcing engineers with trapped-ion or cryogenic control-electronics backgrounds is highly competitive. The team requests early engagement with university recruiting pipelines and specialized job boards.
- **Decision:** People Operations to open the first tranche of 15 control-electronics engineer requisitions by January 6, 2025. Remaining roles to be staged through Q2 2025 based on facility readiness.
- **Action:** Director, People Operations to share updated job descriptions with VP Hardware Engineering for technical review by November 1, 2024.

### 3. Data Retention Policy Update
- **VP Operations** briefed attendees on the revised internal data retention policy, which will take effect December 1, 2024. Key changes include a reduction in the default email archive window from seven years to five years for non-regulated communications, and a new mandatory 90-day purge cycle for ephemeral collaboration channels.
- Legal and IT Security have signed off. All department heads are asked to communicate the changes to their teams and complete acknowledgment forms in Workday by November 15, 2024.
- **Action:** Chief of Staff to distribute updated policy document and acknowledgment link to all attendees by October 22, 2024.

### 4. Intern Program Onboarding Debrief
- **Director, People Operations** shared a brief debrief on the fall intern cohort onboarding. Twelve interns joined across Hardware, Software, and Operations in early October. Overall onboarding satisfaction scores averaged 4.4 out of 5.0.
- Noted gap: lab safety orientation scheduling was compressed due to facility conflicts. A revised onboarding checklist will be piloted for the next cohort.
- **Action:** Director, People Operations to deliver revised intern onboarding checklist to department leads by November 8, 2024.
