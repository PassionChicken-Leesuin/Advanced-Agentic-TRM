## Discussion

### 1. Forte Enterprise On-Premise Specification Update
- Program Manager, Forte Enterprise presented the finalized physical and power envelope for the on-premise Forte Enterprise system: the deployment footprint is two standard 19-inch racks, the system draws approximately 3 kW of power, requires no cryogenics, and delivers 36 algorithmic qubits to the end customer.
- Director of Systems Integration confirmed that the no-cryogenics constraint has been validated through thermal modeling completed in October. This significantly simplifies customer site preparation requirements.
- VP of Hardware noted that the rack and power specs align well with enterprise data-center norms and should accelerate procurement conversations with prospective customers.
- **Decision:** Specification is locked for inclusion in the Q4 2024 customer-facing technical brief. Program Manager to coordinate with Marketing for release language.

### 2. Q4 2024 Vendor Screening — Acousto-Optic Modulators
- Procurement Manager reported that the Q4 2024 vendor screening process for multi-channel acousto-optic modulators has been completed, resulting in a shortlist of three suppliers; one of the shortlisted candidates is a German supplier internally designated as Vendor B.
- Director of Photonics & Laser Systems noted that Vendor B's product line covers the 200–400 MHz RF drive range required for the next-generation trap control architecture and offered favorable lead times.
- Evaluation criteria discussed: channel count scalability, phase coherence specifications, export compliance, and pricing at volume.
- **Decision:** Procurement Manager will issue RFQ packages to all three shortlisted suppliers by 22 November 2024. Technical evaluation panel to include Director of Photonics and one systems engineer.
- Finance Lead flagged that capital expenditure approval for AOM procurement will require a revised spend estimate before the December board reporting cycle.

### 3. Interconnect Tiger Team Formation
- VP of Hardware briefed the group on the plan to stand up a dedicated Interconnect Tiger Team in Q4 2024, comprising eight full-time engineers, with ownership sitting with the VP of Hardware.
- The team's initial charter will focus on photonic interconnect yield, latency benchmarking, and module-to-module signal integrity for multi-QPU configurations.
- CTO expressed support and asked that a 90-day milestone plan be prepared before the team's first sprint begins.
- **Decision:** VP of Hardware to publish team roster and charter document by 22 November 2024. Headcount to be drawn from existing hardware staff plus two open requisitions currently in final-round interviews.

### 4. Cryostat Spare Parts Inventory Review
- Senior Manager, Facilities & Infrastructure reported that current on-hand inventory of cryostat cold-head assemblies stands at two units, which is below the recommended buffer of four given the extended lead times from the primary supplier (currently 14–16 weeks).
- **Decision:** Procurement Manager to place a purchase order for two additional cold-head assemblies before end of November. Finance Lead confirmed budget availability within the existing maintenance OpEx line.
- A secondary review of turbo pump rebuild kits and indium wire stock will be completed by 15 November and results shared via Slack channel #facilities-ops.

### 5. Q4/Q1 Conference Travel Budget
- Finance Lead presented the remaining Q4 conference travel balance: approximately $38,000 unspent across all hardware and engineering cost centers.
- Upcoming events flagged for attendance consideration: Q-NEXT Annual Review (December, Chicago) and IEEE Quantum Week planning session (January, virtual).
- **Decision:** Each director to submit travel requests for Q4 events by 15 November. Unused Q4 funds will not roll over; Q1 2025 travel budget planning cycle opens 2 December.
