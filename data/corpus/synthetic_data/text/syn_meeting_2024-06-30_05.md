## Discussion

### 1. Q2 Sales Pipeline & Lost Deal Analysis

- The Sales Operations Lead presented the Q2 closed-lost summary. **Sales flagged three lost deals in 2024 where the customer cited a competitor's 56-qubit system, raising pressure on raw qubit count as a buying criterion.** The CRO noted this is an emerging pattern that needs a coordinated response across product messaging and roadmap communications.
- VP Hardware Engineering acknowledged that while IonQ's fidelity and algorithmic qubit metrics remain industry-leading, enterprise procurement teams are increasingly using raw qubit count as a shorthand filter in RFP scoring rubrics.
- Director of Product Management to develop updated competitive positioning materials that reframe the conversation around #AQ and circuit performance rather than raw qubit count. Materials should be ready for the sales team by end of Q3.
- CRO requested that future deal reviews explicitly tag lost deals by competitive displacement reason to improve data quality for board-level reporting.

### 2. Customer Success Quarterly Review

- Director of Customer Success presented the Q2 Customer Health Dashboard. Overall NPS trended up 4 points quarter-over-quarter. Four strategic accounts were moved to "Expansion" status following successful proof-of-concept completions.
- One enterprise account in the financial services vertical raised concerns about job queue latency during peak hours on the cloud platform. VP Software & Algorithms confirmed an infrastructure scaling review is already underway and a fix is targeted for mid-July.
- Renewal pipeline for Q3 looks strong; two multi-year renewals are in late-stage negotiation. Director of Customer Success to provide a detailed renewal forecast to Finance Business Partner by July 12.
- The team discussed expanding the quarterly business review (QBR) cadence to include a dedicated technical roadmap briefing for Tier 1 accounts, giving customers earlier visibility into upcoming capabilities.

### 3. Quantum Networking Product Roadmap Update

- Head of Quantum Networking Programs presented the updated roadmap for the networking product line. **A 'quantum networking starter kit' product combining QKD hardware and an ion-photon entanglement node is targeted for 2026.** This offering is intended to lower the barrier for enterprise and government customers to begin deploying end-to-end quantum-secure network segments alongside IonQ compute resources.
- Early conversations with two defense-adjacent partners indicate strong interest in a bundled hardware-plus-services model. Formal go-to-market planning will begin in Q1 2025.
- VP Hardware Engineering flagged that photonic interface yield rates need to improve before the kit can be manufactured at scale; a dedicated yield improvement workstream will be added to the H2 hardware roadmap.

### 4. Conference Travel Budget Review

- Finance Business Partner presented the H2 conference travel forecast. Current approved budget is $340,000 for the second half of 2024, covering IEEE Quantum Week, SC24, and Q2B.
- Three additional speaking invitations have come in for regional events in Europe. The team agreed to approve one (Munich Quantum Summit in October) and defer the others to 2025 given budget constraints.
- All travel requests above $8,000 per event must receive CRO or VP-level sign-off before booking. Finance Business Partner to circulate updated travel policy by July 5.
