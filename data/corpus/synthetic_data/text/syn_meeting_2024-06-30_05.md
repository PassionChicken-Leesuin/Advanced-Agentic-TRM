**1. Q2 Sales Performance & Competitive Landscape Update**

The Sales Director, Enterprise opened with a summary of Q2 pipeline outcomes. Overall bookings were on track against plan, but a concerning pattern was flagged for leadership attention.

- Sales flagged three lost deals in 2024 where the customer explicitly cited a competitor's 56-qubit system as the deciding factor, indicating that raw qubit count is increasingly becoming a front-line buying criterion in competitive evaluations.
- The Chief Revenue Officer noted this is not yet a systemic trend but warrants monitoring. The VP Hardware Engineering confirmed that roadmap milestones are progressing but cautioned against repositioning messaging solely around qubit count without emphasizing IonQ's fidelity and algorithmic qubit advantages.
- Action: Sales Director and VP Software & Algorithms to co-develop a competitive response brief by **July 19, 2024**, addressing qubit count objections with fidelity and performance benchmarking data.
- Action: Director of Product Management to assess whether any near-term system configurations can be highlighted in customer-facing materials to close the perceived gap.

---

**2. Customer Success Quarterly Review**

The Director of Customer Success presented the Q2 customer health summary.

- 14 active enterprise accounts reviewed; 11 rated Green, 2 Yellow, 1 Red.
- The Red account (pharma sector) has experienced repeated scheduling delays on cloud access; a dedicated support plan is being implemented with a target resolution by end of July.
- Net Promoter Score for the quarter came in at 47, up from 41 in Q1, driven largely by improvements in platform uptime and documentation quality.
- Two expansion conversations are in progress with existing accounts in the financial services vertical.
- Renewal pipeline for Q3 is healthy; no at-risk renewals identified beyond the Red account.
- The CRO commended the Customer Success team on NPS improvement and asked for a formal case study to be drafted from one of the Green accounts for marketing use.
- Action: Director of Customer Success to identify a willing reference customer and brief VP Marketing by **July 12, 2024**.

---

**3. Quantum Networking Product Roadmap Update**

The Head of Quantum Networking Programs provided an update on the networking product portfolio.

- Development is proceeding on a 'quantum networking starter kit' — an integrated product combining QKD hardware and a quantum memory node — which is targeted for commercial availability in 2026.
- Early design reviews have been completed; the team is now entering a phase of component sourcing and prototype integration planning.
- The Director of Product Management raised the need to align the go-to-market strategy for this product with the broader IonQ systems roadmap to avoid channel conflict.
- Action: Head of Quantum Networking Programs to present a detailed milestone plan to the leadership team at the **August 11, 2024** program review.

---

**4. Conference Travel Budget Review**

The Finance Business Partner presented the H2 2024 conference travel forecast.

- Current approved travel budget for H2 stands at $310,000 across all functions.
- Requests submitted to date total $287,000, leaving approximately $23,000 unallocated.
- IEEE Quantum Week (Montreal, September) and SC24 (Atlanta, November) are the two largest line items.
- A request from the VP Hardware Engineering team to send two additional engineers to IEEE Quantum Week was reviewed; Finance Business Partner approved the incremental spend of $8,400 within existing budget.
- Action: All function heads to submit final H2 travel requests to Finance by **July 15, 2024** to allow for early booking discounts.
