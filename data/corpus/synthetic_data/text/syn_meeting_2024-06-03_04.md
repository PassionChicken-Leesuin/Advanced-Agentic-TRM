## Discussion

### 1. Seattle Manufacturing Facility – Phase 2 Update
- Director of Manufacturing Operations provided a status update on the Seattle facility expansion.
- Structural work on the Phase 2 wing is approximately 68% complete. Mechanical and electrical rough-in is on schedule.
- **Phase 2 of the Seattle manufacturing facility, which will house an ISO 6 cleanroom, is targeted for commissioning in Q3 2025.** The Director noted that vendor qualification for cleanroom HVAC filtration units must be finalized no later than Q4 2024 to preserve that timeline.
- VP Hardware flagged a dependency: trap fabrication throughput scaling requires the new cleanroom to be operational before end-of-year 2025 capacity commitments can be met.
- **Decision:** Supply Chain Manager to issue RFQs to at least three qualified HVAC filtration vendors by June 28, 2024.

### 2. Quantum Networking Product Roadmap
- Director of Quantum Networking Programs presented an updated roadmap slide deck.
- Discussion centered on the near-term commercialization strategy for quantum networking offerings.
- **IonQ is targeting a 'quantum networking starter kit' product for 2026, which will bundle QKD hardware together with an ion-photon entanglement node as an integrated offering.** The Director indicated that this product is intended to lower the barrier to entry for enterprise and government customers evaluating quantum-secure communications.
- CTO emphasized that the ion-photon entanglement node fidelity benchmarks must reach internal threshold (>90% Bell-state fidelity) before the product can be committed to a firm ship date within 2026.
- VP Hardware confirmed that photonic interface component sourcing discussions are ongoing with two prospective suppliers.
- **Decision:** Director of Quantum Networking Programs to circulate a draft product requirements document (PRD) for the starter kit to all stakeholders by June 21, 2024.

### 3. Cryostat Spare Parts Inventory Review
- Supply Chain Manager reported that current on-hand stock of dilution refrigerator cold-finger assemblies stands at four units, against a minimum buffer target of six.
- Two units are currently allocated to the Forte system at the Bothell lab; one is reserved for the College Park testbed.
- Lead time from the primary cryostat supplier remains approximately 14–16 weeks, which the group agreed creates unacceptable risk given active system deployments.
- **Decision:** Supply Chain Manager authorized to place a purchase order for three additional cold-finger assemblies immediately, targeting delivery before August 31, 2024. VP Hardware to approve PO by June 7, 2024.
- Additionally, turbo pump rebuild kits are at adequate stock levels (12 units on hand); no action required.

### 4. SDK v1.4 Release Notes & Readiness
- Head of Developer Experience walked through the draft release notes for SDK v1.4, currently in internal QA.
- Key changes include: updated noise model API supporting per-gate error injection, expanded Qiskit and Cirq transpiler compatibility, and a new circuit batching interface that reduces job submission overhead by approximately 30% in benchmarks.
- One open issue remains: a known edge case in the transpiler when circuit depth exceeds 500 layers under the Cirq backend. Fix is in review; targeted for merge by June 10, 2024.
- **Decision:** SDK v1.4 external release is tentatively scheduled for June 17, 2024, contingent on transpiler fix validation. Head of Developer Experience to confirm go/no-go by June 12.
