## Discussion

### 1. Seattle Manufacturing Facility – Phase 2 Status Update
- **Director, Manufacturing Operations** provided a progress report on the Seattle expansion. Construction milestones for the Phase 2 build-out remain on schedule. Phase 2 of the Seattle manufacturing facility includes an ISO 6 cleanroom and is targeted for commissioning in Q3 2025.
- Procurement of HEPA filtration units and laminar flow benches is approximately 70% complete. Lead times on remaining equipment are being monitored closely.
- **Program Manager, Facilities** flagged a potential 6-week delay on one vendor's particle counter delivery; escalation to the Supply Chain Manager is in progress.
- **Decision:** COO approved a contingency budget allocation of up to $120K to qualify a secondary vendor for cleanroom monitoring equipment to protect the Q3 2025 commissioning target.
- **Action Item:** Supply Chain Manager to identify and qualify a backup particle counter supplier by **June 21, 2024**.

### 2. Quantum Networking Product Roadmap
- **Director, Quantum Networking Programs** presented an update on the networking product portfolio. The team is formalizing a new offering referred to as the quantum networking starter kit, a product that combines QKD hardware and a quantum memory node into a single integrated package, with a target availability date of 2026.
- Discussion centered on the go-to-market approach: initial customers are expected to be government and financial sector partners. The Director noted that the memory node integration is the critical path item and that a design freeze is planned for Q2 2025.
- **VP Hardware Engineering** asked whether the QKD hardware component would leverage existing photonic interconnect work or require a new supplier engagement. Director confirmed a hybrid approach is under evaluation.
- **Decision:** A dedicated cross-functional working group covering Hardware, Software, and Business Development will be stood up by **June 17, 2024** to own the starter kit roadmap and ensure 2026 readiness.
- **Action Item:** Director, Quantum Networking Programs to circulate a working group charter draft by **June 14, 2024**.

### 3. Cryostat Spare Parts Inventory Review
- **Supply Chain Manager** presented the current spare parts status for deployed cryostat systems. Several consumable categories including indium wire seals, conflat gaskets, and turbomolecular pump service kits are below the minimum stock threshold.
- Two pump service kits are on backorder with an estimated arrival of late July 2024. The team agreed that current on-hand stock is sufficient to cover one unplanned maintenance event before replenishment arrives.
- **Decision:** Minimum stock levels for indium seals will be revised upward from 20 to 35 units given the increased system deployment cadence. Supply Chain Manager to update the inventory management system accordingly.
- **Action Item:** Supply Chain Manager to place an emergency order for two additional turbomolecular pump service kits and confirm delivery timeline by **June 10, 2024**.

### 4. SDK Release Notes & Upcoming Version Planning
- **VP Software & SDK** reviewed the release notes for SDK v1.4.2, which shipped on May 29, 2024. Key changes include improved error handling for job submission timeouts, a new circuit optimization pass for native gate decomposition, and updated Python 3.12 compatibility.
- One known issue was flagged: a regression in the noise model serialization pathway affecting users on Windows. A patch release (v1.4.3) is targeted for **June 12, 2024**.
- Planning for v1.5.0 is underway, with primary features being a new benchmarking module and expanded support for hybrid classical-quantum workflows.
- **Action Item:** VP Software & SDK to share the v1.5.0 feature scope document with the leadership team for review by **June 24, 2024**.
