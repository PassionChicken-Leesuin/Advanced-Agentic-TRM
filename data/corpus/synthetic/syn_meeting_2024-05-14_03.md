## Discussion

### 1. Strategic Customer Pipeline Updates

**Financial Services Vertical**
- The Account Lead for the financial-services engagement presented a requirements update received last week. The customer has formally stated that their target application is portfolio optimization at 120 qubits, and internal consensus among the Applications and Hardware teams is that this scale of computation will require error-corrected logical qubits rather than raw physical qubits to achieve the fidelity levels the customer needs.
- VP Software & Algorithms noted that mapping the portfolio optimization workload to logical qubits changes the resource-estimation picture significantly and that a revised technical proposal should be prepared before the next customer touchpoint, currently scheduled for early June.
- CRO asked that the Account Lead loop in the Director of Quantum Applications to co-author the proposal. **Agreed.**

**Automotive Vertical**
- Director, Quantum Applications briefed the group on the status of the automotive engagement. The customer has communicated a roadmap expectation that battery-chemistry simulations will require more than 80 qubits by 2026, which aligns with the projected scale-up trajectory on IonQ's hardware roadmap but leaves limited margin for slippage.
- VP Hardware Engineering confirmed that current internal projections support hitting that threshold within the window, but emphasized that the schedule is sensitive to supply-chain lead times on trap components.
- Action assigned to VP Hardware Engineering to provide a formal schedule-risk assessment by 2024-05-31.

### 2. Roadmap Alignment
- VP Hardware Engineering gave a brief update on the Forte-class system integration progress. No major blockers reported; next internal milestone is a gate-fidelity benchmark review scheduled for the week of June 3.
- VP Software & Algorithms raised the need to align compiler stack updates with the hardware milestone calendar. A joint working session between the Hardware and Software teams will be scheduled for the week of May 27.

### 3. Q3 Conference Travel Budget
- Finance Business Partner presented the preliminary Q3 travel budget. Total allocation proposed is $185,000, covering IEEE Quantum Week (September, Montreal), Q2B (scheduled for October, but pre-commitments fall in Q3), and two customer-facing industry events.
- CRO requested a 10% contingency line be added given the possibility of a late-breaking invitation to present at a European venue. Finance Business Partner will revise and recirculate by May 21.
- Group agreed that all travel requests above $8,000 per trip require VP-level sign-off going forward.

### 4. Software Engineering Hiring Pipeline
- Director, Talent Acquisition reported that the team currently has 11 open software engineering requisitions across compiler engineering, cloud platform, and SDK development.
- Of the 11 reqs, 4 are at final-round interview stage, with offers expected to go out before May 31 for at least 3 of those roles.
- A concern was raised about candidate drop-off at the technical screen stage; Director, Talent Acquisition will work with VP Software & Algorithms to review and calibrate the take-home assessment rubric by May 24.
- Headcount plan for the remainder of 2024 targets a net addition of 18 software engineers; current pipeline is tracking to approximately 60% of that goal.

3. Joint Hardware–Software roadmap working session to be scheduled for the week of May 27.
