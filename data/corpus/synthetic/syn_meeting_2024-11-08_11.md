### 1. Q4 Strategic Acquisition Screening Update

**Owner: VP of Business Development**

- The VP of Business Development reported that the Q4 2024 screening process has shortlisted three acquisition candidates focused on electronic qubit control technology, including a UK-based startup referred to internally as Candidate B.
- Legal and finance have been engaged to begin preliminary due-diligence scoping for all three candidates.
- Candidate B was noted as particularly interesting given its IP position around low-latency FPGA-based control electronics; the BD team is coordinating a technical review with the CTO's office.
- **Decision:** CTO to assign two senior engineers to conduct a technical deep-dive on Candidate B by November 22, 2024. NDA execution to be prioritized by Legal this week.
- **Action Item:** VP of Business Development to circulate updated candidate summary deck to leadership by November 15, 2024.

### 2. Interconnect Tiger Team Formation

**Owner: VP of Hardware**

- The VP of Hardware confirmed that an Interconnect Tiger Team consisting of eight full-time engineers will be formed in Q4 2024, with ownership sitting directly under the VP of Hardware.
- The team's initial mandate will cover photonic and electrical interconnect challenges relevant to multi-module scaling.
- Headcount sourcing: five positions will be filled by internal transfers; three will require new hires. Recruiting has been briefed.
- **Decision:** Tiger Team charter document to be finalized and distributed by November 20, 2024.
- **Action Item:** VP of Hardware to confirm internal transfer nominations with respective engineering managers by November 14, 2024. Director of Finance to confirm budget allocation for three open headcount by November 15, 2024.

### 3. Forte Enterprise On-Premise Specification Review

**Owner: VP of Hardware / VP of Software Engineering**

- The team reviewed the current approved specification for the Forte Enterprise on-premise deployment configuration: the system fits within two standard 19-inch racks, consumes approximately 3 kW of power, requires no cryogenics, and delivers 36 algorithmic qubits.
- The no-cryogenics requirement was highlighted as a key differentiator enabling standard data-center deployment without facility modifications.
- VP of Software Engineering noted that the 36 algorithmic qubit target aligns with the planned circuit benchmarking suite for enterprise customer pilots in H1 2025.
- **Decision:** Specification is locked for current production planning cycle. Any change requests must go through the formal ECO process.
- **Action Item:** Program Operations Lead to update the Forte Enterprise product spec sheet and distribute to Sales Engineering by November 18, 2024.
